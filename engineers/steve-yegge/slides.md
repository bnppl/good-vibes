### A replacement needs a reference

> **New green tests can agree with a new mistake.**
>
> Recommendation · published 2025-12-07. Yegge’s December 2025 advice describes regenerating tests after changes. In our adaptation, preserving an independent acceptance contract is how a rewrite can be checked against the original intent.
