## Scope and dates

Publication window: **1 October 2025–26 October 2026**. Publication, practice and inspection dates are separate. This page prioritises actual use where documented; recommendations and inspected artifacts have their own labels. A newly published retrospective may describe older work.

## Porting MiniJinja, then composing tools in Pi

In June 2026 Ronacher reports successful loop-assisted porting of MiniJinja to Go while describing difficulties accepting unattended long-lived code. In October he publishes examples from his own Pi sessions using Codemode to compose operations, alongside an explanation of the harness/execution boundary.

[S09](https://lucumr.pocoo.org/2026/6/23/the-coming-loop/) · [S12](https://lucumr.pocoo.org/2026/10/6/codemode/)

## Limits and background

The task distinction matters: successful translation and experimental exploration do not establish satisfaction with unsupervised maintenance. His July 2025 test-generation complaints are historical, not his current testing policy.

The optional reward exercise in the earlier research/workflow remains our proposed adaptation. It is not the reported project above or evidence that the engineer follows the proposed sequence.
