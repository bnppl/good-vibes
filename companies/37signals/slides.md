## Card 01

### Lexxy’s browser loop

> **Give the agent the interaction that exposes the bug.**
>
> Reported team use · published 2026-09-28. The editor account describes switching to Playwright for more realistic feedback.

**In the wild:** Claude-assisted Lexxy work could exercise clipboard, focus and keyboard behavior.

**Takeaway:** Choose checks that observe the user’s actual interaction.

### The wrong sandbox

> **A production sandbox is not a coding-agent policy.**
