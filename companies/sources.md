## Scope and access

The collection targets publications/revisions from **1 October 2025–26 October 2026**. An eligible publication may describe earlier work; the practice period remains visible. Source inspection does not establish a complete current private configuration, successful reproduction, observed compliance or organisation-wide adoption. A public policy is labelled as policy. Official source summaries, first-person team accounts, papers, artifacts and recommendations retain different evidence labels.

Open-source code supplies inspectable engineering background. It does not prove that the described private harness is publicly available, or that an old public project was built using AI. GitHub/Microsoft share ownership; community projects may outlive or move beyond their company of origin. Licences and component boundaries are not interchangeable.

CircleCI customer telemetry is not CircleCI’s own internal development method. JPMorgan strategy statements do not establish release controls or an autonomous workflow. Detailed unknowns remain explicit in those profiles. The initial shortlist’s weaker Coinbase and 37signals examples have been expanded with project-specific evidence; GitLab’s dated public policy remains distinct from actual compliance.

No full copyrighted publication or inaccessible recording is archived. The registers contain concise findings and original links. These are bounded public-evidence studies, not comprehensive corporate audits.
