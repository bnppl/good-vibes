# How companies build software using AI

[All visualisations →]({{ '/visualisations/' | relative_url }}){: .btn .btn-primary }



[Explore the pipeline atlas: tools, skills and processes]({{ '/pipeline/' | relative_url }}){: .btn }

Twenty company knowledge bases across banking, payments, crypto, education, retail, media, transport, infrastructure and developer tools. Each keeps detailed research, source findings, an observed workflow, optional practice and bite-size slides together in its own directory.

The focus is **using AI to build, verify and maintain software**, with primary publications/revisions since **1 October 2025** and a research horizon of **26 October 2026**. Publication, practice and inspection dates remain separate. Sources describe specific teams, policies or cases; they do not establish one timeless method for every employee.

These are suggested reading routes, not exclusive categories or an engineering-quality ranking. Open-source contributions and substantive engineering writing make the cohort inspectable; reputation is not proof of a particular current AI workflow. Foundation/community governance, open core and historical releases remain labelled in the research.
