# Mozilla — small ideas from reported practice

Window: 1 October 2025–26 October 2026. Each card names its evidence kind and date. [Detailed research](research.md) retains the scope and limits.

## Card 01

### The actionable report

> **A minimal reproducer changes the maintainer’s job.**
>
> Reported team use · published 2026-03-06. Mozilla could validate external findings against actual execution.

**In the wild:** Collaboration in weeks before Firefox 148 release. External discovery and Mozilla validation have different attribution; no independent re-execution here.

**Takeaway:** Ask what observation would distinguish a real bug from a plausible story.

## Card 03

### The pipeline

> **Finding more bugs does not ship more fixes by itself.**
>
> Reported team use · published 2026-05-07. Mozilla connects investigation to triage, patching and release work.

**In the wild:** Security work after February 2026 collaboration and before publication. Security-hardening account; internal findings are grouped into CVEs, so bug and CVE totals differ.

**Takeaway:** Study the full handling path around the model.
