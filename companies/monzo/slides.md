# Monzo: bite-sized ideas

Derived from the dated source findings checked on 26 October 2026. Team/company reports, background and our optional practice remain separate.

## Card 01

### A real small change

> **A copy mismatch can reveal the whole delivery boundary.**
>
> Reported team use · published 2026-03-20. The March designer account describes fixing inconsistent wording across product surfaces.

**In the wild:** A designer works with engineering rather than bypassing review.

**Takeaway:** Study a complete small change before generalising about autonomy.

## Card 02

### Separate authorities

> **Writing the patch does not grant release authority.**
>
> Reported team use · published 2026-03-20. The March account makes review mandatory and keeps deployment with qualified engineers.

**In the wild:** More people can contribute without every contributor becoming a deployer.

**Takeaway:** Record who may edit, review, merge and release separately.

## Card 03

### Controlled execution

> **A bank can build agent capability around an explicit access boundary.**
>
> Reported company use · published 2026-08-13. The August platform account describes sandboxed bundles and a restricted proxy.

**In the wild:** Permissions can be selected for a task instead of inherited wholesale from a laptop.

**Takeaway:** Inspect the access path, not just the prompt.

### Context is infrastructure

> **The agent needs the organisation’s evidence, not just its source files.**

### A roadmap is not a result

> **Next steps cannot fill a current evidence gap.**
