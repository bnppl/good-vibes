# GitLab — bite-sized ideas

Recent AI-assisted development evidence, scoped to the inspected cases. Publication/revision dates differ from practice dates.

## Card 01

### The date is now inspectable

> **A pinned revision improves provenance.**
>
> Published artifact · March and October 2026. The two handbook chapters now have verified commit identities.

**In the wild:** The earlier undated-source gap is resolved; compliance remains unverified.

**Takeaway:** Date the document without dating every passage or session.

## Card 02

### Policy versus execution

> **A handbook is not a run log.**
>
> Declared policy · October 2026. The playbook specifies an agent-assisted delivery framework.

**In the wild:** Its internal examples are summaries, not independently inspected project histories.

**Takeaway:** Look for executed checks when comparing actual outcomes.

### Who reviews?

> **Independent does not always mean human.**
>
> Declared policy · March/October 2026. DevEx and R&D review wording have different scopes.

**In the wild:** The DevEx requirement cannot silently become a company-wide rule.

**Takeaway:** Name the actor and authority separately.
