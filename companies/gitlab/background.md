## What makes this a useful engineering study

Study transparent team practices; date qualification remains pending. The open-source link provides a concrete place to inspect code, contribution practices and engineering artifacts. It is background for selection, not proof that this AI workflow produced that project or that every team works identically.

## Limits

Team-scoped declared practice, not observed compliance. It is relevant but does not yet meet the strict dated-publication test. A dated revision must be captured before using it in the since-October comparison.

Practice dates are those described in the source, not the date of this review. Exact task dates and the complete current private setup are not established unless stated. These accounts were inspected, not independently executed. Open-source origin, ongoing maintenance and project governance are different relationships; foundation/community projects are labelled accordingly.

## Questions for a deeper comparison

- What intent or contract does the agent receive, and who can change it?
- What executes independently to check the result?
- Who owns review, merge and production release decisions?
- What changed in defect rate, lead time, operational outcomes and cost per completed task?

A product AI feature, a tool capability demo or an adoption percentage alone cannot answer those questions.
